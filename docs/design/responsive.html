Build it with

  • Mobile-first CSS: base styles are the phone, then @media (min-width: 640px) and (min-width: 1024px).
  • One AppShell component owns the nav. Screens only lay out their content with CSS grid.
  • No separate mobile and desktop pages. Same components, same routes.
  • Remove the 480 px .screen cap that frames the app as a phone on desktop.

Touch and mouse

  • Every tap target at least 44 × 44 px on all sizes.
  • Drag and drop only when (pointer: fine) and expanded. Tap to assign always works.
  • Hover states only under (hover: hover).
  • Keyboard: every action reachable with Tab, Enter and Escape. Focus ring always visible.

Content

  • Text runs at most 70 characters wide. Amounts never wrap.
  • Long names and shop names truncate with an ellipsis, full text on hover or in the detail view.
  • Numbers use tabular figures so columns line up.
  • Safe-area insets on phones with notches and home bars.

Check before merging

  • Playwright screenshots at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1600 px, light and dark.
  • No horizontal page scroll at any of those widths.
  • Rotate a phone to landscape: medium layout, nothing cut off.
  • Browser zoom at 200%: still usable.
