Responsive layout

One app for phone, tablet and desktop, with no accounts anywhere. Home is a start page and a list of bills saved in this browser. Inside a bill, you are a name in that bill only. Flat white cards, hairline borders, one teal accent and one icon set.

No accounts, and it shows

Removed

  • "Hi Ana" greeting, profile avatar and the "Me" tab.
  • Notification bell. There is nothing to sign in to, so nothing to notify.
  • "You're owed" totals across bills. Money is shown per bill only.
  • App-wide Settle and Activity. Both live inside each bill.

Instead

  • Home is "Split a bill" plus On this device: bills this browser has opened.
  • Scan takes a new photo or one from the gallery. On desktop: upload or drag photos in.
  • Your name belongs to one bill: "You're Ben · Change" in the bill header.
  • A footnote says where data lives and offers Forget this device.
  • Navigation appears only inside a bill: Items, People, Receipts, Settle.

Look

  • Flat white cards with 1 px borders. Only the receipt card lifts.
  • One accent: deep teal for actions and "you". Amber means unclaimed, green means paid, red means check this.
  • Avatars are neutral grey initials. Yours is teal.
  • Inter for everything, JetBrains Mono for receipt lines.

Icons

  • Lucide only (lucide-vue-next), 1.75 stroke, 20 px, 16 px in chips.
  • Every icon button has a text label or aria-label.
  • No emoji, no mixed icon sets, no filled icons.

Build it with

  • Mobile-first CSS, then @media (min-width: 640px) and (min-width: 1024px).
  • BillShell owns the tab bar, rail and sidebar. Home has no shell nav.
  • Same components and routes at every size.
  • Remove the 480 px .screen cap.

Touch and mouse

  • Every tap target at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Drag and drop only with (pointer: fine) on expanded. Mine and Share always work.
  • Hover styles only under (hover: hover). Focus ring always visible.

Check before merging

  • Screenshots at 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280 and 1600 px, light and dark.
  • No horizontal scroll. Amounts never wrap. Long names truncate.
  • Browser zoom at 200%: still usable.
